DICTATE — THIRD-PARTY DATA & MODEL ATTRIBUTIONS
==============================================

Dictate bundles and/or downloads neural models, logos and fonts from the following
freely licensed sources. Every model listed here runs on your device; none of them send
audio or text anywhere.


1. On-device speech-to-text models (offline transcription)
----------------------------------------------------------
Downloaded on demand into <filesDir>/dictate-models/<model>/ ; no audio ever leaves the device.

One-shot models — these transcribe after you stop speaking:

  - Whisper (tiny/base/small, multilingual and English) — © OpenAI, MIT License.
    https://github.com/openai/whisper
  - Parakeet Ultra — a post-train of NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 covering the same 25 European
    languages, © moondream, licensed CC BY 4.0. https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra
  - Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 — © NVIDIA, licensed CC BY 4.0.
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
  - Parakeet TDT 110M (English) — © NVIDIA, licensed CC BY 4.0.
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m
  - Parakeet German (primeline) — a German-specialized fine-tune of NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3,
    © primeline, licensed CC BY 4.0. https://huggingface.co/primeline/parakeet-primeline
  - FastConformer German — © NVIDIA, licensed CC BY 4.0.
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/stt_de_fastconformer_hybrid_large_pc
  - Canary 180M Flash — © NVIDIA, licensed CC BY 4.0.
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/canary-180m-flash
  - GigaAM v2 and v3 Russian — © Salute Devices / GigaChat Team, MIT License.
    https://github.com/salute-developers/GigaAM
  - Dolphin base (40 Eastern languages: Hindi, Arabic, Persian, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian,
    Bengali, Tamil, Urdu and more) — © DataoceanAI and Tsinghua University, Apache-2.0.
    https://github.com/DataoceanAI/Dolphin

  - SenseVoice Small (Chinese, Cantonese, English, Japanese, Korean) — © Alibaba Group,

    modification and sharing with attribution of the source, the author and the model name.

2. Supporting on-device models
------------------------------

  - Silero VAD — detects speech, used to segment long recordings and to skip silent ones.
    © Silero Team, MIT License. https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad
  - Smart Turn v3 — decides whether a spoken thought is finished, for long-form auto-split.
    © Pipecat AI, licensed BSD-2-Clause. https://github.com/pipecat-ai/smart-turn

3. Model runtime and conversions
--------------------------------

The ONNX (int8) exports of every model above, and the on-device inference runtime, come from the
sherpa-onnx / k2-fsa project, Apache-2.0. https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
Inference runs through ONNX Runtime, © Microsoft, MIT License. https://onnxruntime.ai

All of the above permit redistribution; the files are mirrored on the project's own GitHub release
for a stable, project-controlled source.

4. Provider logos
-----------------

The settings list marks each AI provider with its own logo, so a row can be found by its mark rather
than read. The marks are used only to identify the service the row configures; every trademark
remains the property of its owner, and their use here implies no affiliation or endorsement.

The monochrome outlines come from two collections, both freely licensed:

  - Simple Icons, CC0-1.0 — OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Mistral AI, ElevenLabs,
    Deepgram, DeepSeek, Ollama, Microsoft Azure, Scaleway, OVHcloud.
    https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
  - Lobe Icons, MIT License — Groq, Together AI, DeepInfra, AssemblyAI, xAI, SiliconFlow (filed
    there under SiliconCloud, the name of their platform).
    https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons

Soniox publish no vector mark, so theirs was traced from their own app icon and fitted to the same
size as the rest.
